Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Data Collection & Contact Form Processing

VIBE.STUDIO values your privacy. When you send a message through the contact form, the details you submit (name, email address, message topic, budget, and project context) are securely processed via Web3Forms strictly to evaluate and respond to your business inquiry.

No contact details or message content are sold, leased, shared with third-party advertisers, or used for automated marketing mailing lists without your explicit consent.

2. Analytics & Cookies

This website prioritizes privacy-first design. We do not place non-essential tracking cookies or run invasive cross-site profiling scripts. Any telemetry used is strictly limited to privacy-respecting client event monitoring (e.g. CTA interactions and language selection) with zero storage of personal identifiers.

3. IP Logging & Security Monitoring

Standard server access logs (including IP address, user-agent string, timestamp, and requested page route) are automatically processed by our web host (Vercel / Cloudflare) to ensure platform security, mitigate automated rate-limit abuse, prevent DDoS attacks, and maintain web application firewall rules.

4. Your Rights & Data Contact

If you have questions about your privacy or wish to request the deletion of any correspondence sent via the contact form, please email privacy@yourstudio.com.
